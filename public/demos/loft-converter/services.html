Our Services

Loft conversions built to last a lifetime.

D From £35,000

Dormer Loft Conversion

From £35,000

The most popular conversion type in Birmingham. A full-width or box dormer extends the rear roofline upward, delivering full standing height across the entire floorplate. Perfect for terraces and semis where every square metre counts. Most projects deliver a master bedroom plus en-suite or two double rooms within the new space.

We handle the full planning process, structural calculations, party wall agreements, and the build itself — start to finish, one fixed price.

  • Full planning service included
  • Structural calculations included
  • Choice of window styles
  • En-suite option available
  • 10 year guarantee
H From £45,000

Hip to Gable Conversion

From £45,000

For detached and semi-detached homes with a hipped (sloping) end roof, converting the slope into a vertical gable wall transforms the available volume. Combined with a rear dormer, this gives the maximum possible loft footprint — typically a master suite with dressing room and en-suite, or two large double bedrooms.

The architectural impact is significant; we work closely with planning officers to ensure the new gable sits comfortably within the streetscape.

  • Party wall agreement handled
  • Maximum space gain
  • Velux or dormer window options
  • Full interior fit out available
  • 10 year guarantee
V From £28,000

Velux Loft Conversion

From £28,000

The cleanest, fastest, and most cost-effective route into your loft. Roof windows preserve the existing roofline entirely — meaning no planning permission is usually required and the build is dramatically less invasive. Ideal for homes with sufficient existing head height and a generous loft footprint.

Light flooding through pitched-roof windows transforms a dark roof void into one of the most desirable rooms in the house.

  • No planning permission usually required
  • Fastest build time — 8 to 10 weeks
  • Minimal disruption to household
  • FAKRO or Velux windows specified
  • 10 year guarantee
S From £1,500

Loft Storage and Boarding

From £1,500

Not every loft is suited to a full conversion — but every loft can be made dramatically more useful. Our boarding service delivers a properly engineered, structurally sound storage platform with a fixed loft ladder, power, and lighting. Insulation is checked and topped up as part of the visit.

The fastest, simplest upgrade we offer — typically completed in two days.

  • Structural boarding
  • Fixed loft ladder
  • Power and lighting
  • Insulation check included
