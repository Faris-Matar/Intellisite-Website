Common Questions

Before you book the survey.

Do I need planning permission?
Most Velux conversions and many dormer conversions fall under permitted development and do not require a full planning application. Hip to gable conversions and front-facing dormers usually do. We assess your specific property at the survey and handle the entire planning process if it is needed — included in the fixed price.
How long does a loft conversion take?
Velux conversions typically take 8 to 10 weeks on site. Dormer conversions run 12 to 14 weeks. Hip to gable conversions are usually 14 to 16 weeks. Add 6 to 10 weeks before that for planning permission if it is required. We give you a precise programme as part of the fixed-price quote.
Will I need to move out during the build?
No. Loft conversions are designed to allow you to live in your home throughout the build — most of the structural work happens above your existing ceiling. There is some disruption when the staircase is cut in, but the team works to minimise it and we keep you informed at every stage.
What is included in the fixed price?
Everything required to deliver the conversion to a complete, signed-off finish: planning, structural calculations, party wall agreements, the build itself, all electrics and plumbing, plastering, decoration, flooring, and final building control inspection. The only things not included are the contents — furniture, fittings of your choice, and any external landscaping.
Do you offer finance options?
Yes. We work with two specialist home-improvement lenders who can fund loft conversions over 5 or 10 year terms. Most clients prefer a remortgage or savings, but finance is available if it suits your circumstances. We can introduce you at the quote stage — there is no obligation to proceed.
